# Tier B Renderers

**Modes:** `blogpost`, `slides`

In Tier B, Minto controls the information architecture — the sequence and hierarchy of what is said — while the surface style follows the conventions of the format. The argument must survive the format, not the other way around.

---

## `/minto blogpost`

Use for editorial, educational, thought-leadership, and SEO-oriented articles. The goal is to answer the reader's central question early, then expand through logically ordered explanation, evidence, examples, and nuance.

Identify first: the reader or search question, the Governing Thought, the Key Lines, the supporting explanations, and the evidence or examples.

```text
Reader Question / Search Intent
↓
Core Answer
↓
Key Line 1 → H2
   ├── Support → H3
   ├── Evidence
   └── Example
Key Line 2 → H2 …
↓
Implication / Recommendation
↓
Conclusion
```

* Do not write a long generic introduction before answering the topic. The reader arrived with a question.
* Derive headings from the logical argument, never from keywords alone.
* H2s should correspond to Key Lines; H3s to supporting ideas.
* Avoid repeating the same conclusion in multiple sections — this is the most common structural failure in long-form content.
* Preserve nuance wherever a simple answer would be misleading.
* For SEO tasks, optimize for the search intent without sacrificing logical coherence. Intent and structure usually agree; when they conflict, coherence wins.
* Do not add filler purely for word count.

---

## `/minto slides`

Use for presentations, decks, and slide storylines. The defining test: **the sequence of slide titles should itself communicate the argument.**

* Use message titles, not topic titles.
* Each slide communicates one principal message.
* Charts and evidence exist to prove that slide's message — if a chart proves nothing stated in the title, either the title or the chart is wrong.
* The slide sequence follows the pyramid's logic.
* Put supporting detail in the body, never in the title.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Bad title | Conversion Rate by Device |
| Better title | Mobile drives nearly all of the conversion deterioration |
| Bad title | Recommendations |
| Better title | Fixing the mobile PDP should precede further media scaling |

A reader should understand the entire storyline by reading only the slide titles, in order, without seeing a single chart.
